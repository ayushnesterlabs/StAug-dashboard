Identity and lifecycle

Person, applications, screening, and employment

target-state-data-model-core.svg
Purpose

Explains the stable Person identity, independent application records, employment episodes, changeable employee numbers, and durable separation/reinstatement/rehire history.

How to read it

Person is the identity anchor. A Person can have repeated EmploymentEpisodes. Employee numbers belong to an episode and never identify the human. Applications remain separate and are not automatically converted to Person records.

Decision boundary

Resolved D3 controls stable identity and no automatic matching. Resolved D4 controls separation, reinstatement, and rehire behavior. Screening configuration remains under D18.

Employee operations

Workforce history, benefits, leave, and contacts

target-state-data-model-operations.svg
Purpose

Shows the repeatable histories and events that sit around an employment episode or person: work placement, schedule, status, pay, evaluations, benefits, leave, credentials, and contacts.

How to read it

Assignments preserve changing conditions over time instead of overwriting Person. Leave remains person-centered; benefits and most workforce histories use the EmploymentEpisode as their employment context.

Decision boundary

Exact status-change, FMLA, benefit, emergency-contact, reporting, and organization rules remain governed by D5, D6, D8, D11, D12, and D19.

Boundary overview

Paper, security, and external/manual boundaries

target-state-data-model-boundaries.svg
Purpose

Makes the non-digital and external edges visible instead of implying that the platform owns every record or can integrate every external system.

How to read it

Dashed boundary links mean a human handoff, lookup, manual entry, print, fax, mail, phone call, or prescribed output—not an API or synchronization.

Decision boundary

Paper and prescribed forms remain where required. Digital document scope, interfaces, security, retention, and external templates remain open.

Manual workforce systems

Workforce external/manual detail

target-state-data-model-boundaries-workforce.svg
Purpose

Separates payroll, Kronos, OnShift, scheduling, DoorCard, SNF Clinic, KnowBe4, and Ohio New Hire Reporting into distinct operational boundaries.

How to read it

Each endpoint has only the handoffs supported by discovery. Keeping endpoints separate prevents a grouped box from falsely implying that every system participates in every workflow.

Decision boundary

No connector, synchronization, credentials, automated status truth, or source-of-truth ownership is asserted.

Manual benefits systems

Benefits and vendor boundary detail

target-state-data-model-boundaries-benefits.svg
Purpose

Distinguishes Sun Life census delivery from disability claims, and keeps Diocese/Paycor, Principal, Colonial Life, payroll, and related handoffs separate.

How to read it

The model treats a handoff or observed vendor status as evidence of work—not proof that the vendor completed or accepted the underlying business action.

Decision boundary

Required files, formulas, statuses, delivery cadence, and interfaces await approved samples and owner sign-off under D6, D9, and D13.

Controlled evidence

Paper records and security detail

target-state-data-model-boundaries-paper-security.svg
Purpose

Keeps personnel and medical physical files, paper document occurrences, accounts, groups, memberships, sensitive classifications, and audit events conceptually distinct.

How to read it

Business events such as SeparationEvent remain durable history and are not replaced by TechnicalAuditEvent records. ApplicationUserAccount remains separate from HR Person, with no approved automatic link.

Decision boundary

The field-level permission matrix, export rights, authentication, event dictionary, retention, alerts, and hosting rules remain open under D2, D7, D14, and D17.
